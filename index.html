What we offer

Transportation matched to how you actually need to travel

Every rider is different. Our staff and vehicles are equipped to meet you at your ability level — not the other way around.

Ambulatory

Direct, hands-on assistance getting in and out of the vehicle, navigating stairs, and moving past obstacles along the way.

Wheelchair

Trained staff and ADA-compliant vehicles accommodate manual and electric wheelchairs and scooters of varying size.

Gurney & Stretcher

Stretcher-accessible vans with Stryker stretchers and trained attendants, serving hospitals and facility partners.

Medical Courier

Same-day delivery supporting facility supply chains — records, medication, supplies, equipment, and linens.

Our story
"We built Gold Mountain Transport because I saw, firsthand, how much our aging neighbors needed more than a ride — they needed someone in their corner."

Ramases Gousheh · Founder, Gold Mountain Transport

A licensed and certified flight and ambulance paramedic with more than a decade of experience, Ramases spent years supporting hospital and emergency room staff and caring for patients through every kind of injury and trauma. That work made one thing clear: our growing senior population, and anyone facing mobility challenges, needed a more timely and compassionate transportation option.

Ramases and his wife, Andrea, launched Gold Mountain Transport to be exactly that — a company focused on exceptional, timely service and genuine compassion, not just volume. Today GMT is headquartered in Loomis, CA, an Equal Opportunity Employer, a certified Minority Business Enterprise, and a proud member of the United Medical Transportation Providers Group.

Frequently asked questions

A few things riders and families often ask before booking. Don’t see your question? Give dispatch a call.

What areas do you serve?

We provide local, door-through-door service throughout Placer, Sacramento, and Nevada Counties, along with pre-scheduled trips statewide for longer-distance appointments.

What types of rides do you offer?

We offer ambulatory, wheelchair, gurney & stretcher, and medical courier transportation, matched to the rider’s mobility needs for each trip.

How do I schedule a ride?

Call our dispatch team at (530) 485-5113 or send a ride request by email, and we’ll follow up to confirm pickup, destination, timing, and pricing.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept card, check, and cash. New riders also receive a discount off their first ambulatory or wheelchair round trip.

Do you provide same-day or urgent trips?

We recommend booking as early as possible, but our dispatch team can often accommodate same-day requests, including hospital discharges — just call ahead so we can check availability.
